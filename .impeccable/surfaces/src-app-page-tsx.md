# Surface: CARE+ landing and app shell

Mode: Persuade (landing `/`), Operate (app `/app/*`). Audience: Thai teens and working adults with risky habits who feel fine today. Action: start the 3-minute health check, or open the sample result.

Pinned by the user: the PeelPlan world (their own project), CARE+ blue as the one accent, the robot mascot prominent across the app, no emoji. Previous pastel-gradient look is the anti-reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The future-health chart is the product object, shown at working scale and touchable in the first viewport, the way PeelPlan shows the real lid with rulers. Refuses the split hero with a feature-card grid and every gradient, pastel tile, and emoji.

OWN-WORLD: Pure white ground, near-black ink #0b0b0f, grey second voice #6b6d76, hairlines #e8e8ec, faint graph-paper grid behind instruments. One accent, CARE+ blue #1f6fff: primary pill with an ink outline ring, hand-drawn underline, the "if you change" line. "Keep habits" is drawn in ink. Anuphan bold display, grey second line. Lucide icons in small pastel squares only. Robot avatar as guide.

STORY: Visitor sees two futures from one starting point, flips habits and watches the blue line fall, believes small changes matter, starts the check.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav. Left: two-line Thai headline, second phrase underlined in blue, grey sub, blue pill CTA, note. Right: chart instrument on grid with year ruler, persona chips, habit toggles that redraw the blue line, robot with a live bubble.

FORM: user-pinned PeelPlan world; concept-seed was not run because the user pinned the direction. seed: none (direction pinned by user, roll not run)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
