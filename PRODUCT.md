# Product

> Source: The Challengers, PSAT HealthHacks 2026 pitch deck (72-hour hackathon round).

## Platform

Web, mobile-first (the deck shows a phone app; the web app uses a bottom tab bar on phones and a sidebar on desktop).

## Users

- Teens and working adults with risky habits (short sleep, sweet/salty/fatty food, little exercise, stress) who do not yet feel any effect.
- People with NCD risk factors who need a nudge and a clear next step.
- Later: families, schools, companies, hospitals and clinics (B2B).

## Purpose

Close the gap between "knowing I should be healthy" and "seeing why it matters and acting on it", by showing the link between today's behaviour and future health.

## Positioning

Other health apps record data and show history. CARE+ analyses behaviour against NCD risk, simulates the future with and without change, gives personalised small actions, and routes to care when needed.

## Principles

1. Never diagnose. Say "พบปัจจัยเสี่ยง" or "ควรตรวจเพิ่มเติม", and send people to a doctor when warranted.
2. Make change visible: every recommendation links back to a factor and forward to a simulated outcome.
3. Small, doable actions over generic advice.
4. Private by default: collect only what's needed, with consent, user-controlled.
5. Simple enough for older users.

## Brand

Name CARE+. Bright, friendly, light UI matching the prototype screens: airy blue background, white cards, one colour per feature (chat blue, analysis purple, simulation green, plan orange, see-a-doctor pink), and the CARE+ robot mascot from the app icon. Thai-first copy. Anuphan for Thai and Latin, Instrument Serif italic as an occasional accent. Light theme only (the prototype has no dark mode), WCAG AA, reduced motion respected.

## Evidence on hand

WHO Thailand NCDs Investment Case: NCDs cause ~74% of deaths in Thailand (~400,000 per year) and cost ~1.6 trillion baht a year (~9.7% of 2019 GDP). No user research, clinical validation, or real facility data yet.
